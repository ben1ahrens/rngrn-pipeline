## Mandatory caveats (verbatim-ready)

- **c2_P is the TUNING half of the §1a split.** `docs/PREREGISTRATION.md` §1a fixes
  `three_gene_qvar` `sample_0000`–`sample_0007` as TUNING (burned) and `sample_0008`–`0033`
  as HELD OUT. c2_P's two targets are `sample_0001` and `sample_0004` — both inside the
  burned tuning range. **Any number from this campaign is a tuning number, not held-out
  evidence for a §3 pass condition.**
- **Targets are `three_gene_qvar`**, superseded as the primary training-data source by the
  canonical `turing_spots`/`turing_labyrinth` sets (`docs/DECISIONS.md` D-CANON-4,
  2026-08-10). `three_gene_qvar`'s own numbers are not withdrawn — it is the provenance of
  the canonical systems and every existing result against it stands — but it no longer
  supplies new headline claims.
- **Best-of-64 restarts.** Every run in both the campaign and the D5 diagnostic reports the
  winner of 64 independent restarts (`n_restarts_requested = n_restarts_run = 64` in every
  c2_P row); the D5 contrast figure exists precisely because that restart selection is not
  uniform across seeds even against one fixed target.
- **2 targets x 8 seeds = 16 runs.** `dataset_id = three_gene_qvar`, `sample_key ∈
  {sample_0001, sample_0004}`, `seed ∈ {0..7}` for each, `form = nc1`, all `arm =
  fully_observed`.

## Figure 3 — D5 contrast

`figures/3_d5_contrast.png`, source `experiments/diag_fft/d5/` (this worktree; part of the
lift-ladder diagnostics, `docs/DIAGNOSTICS_lift.md` D5).

- 10 seeds (0-9) recovering the **same** target (`kstar_fft_true = 0.28868`), best-of-64
  restarts each, differing only in restart/init seed.
- **4 interior-k\* successes** — seeds 1, 3, 5, 6: `kstar_model = 0.28476` (identical across
  all four to displayed precision), `kstar_fft_rel_err = 0.01359` (well under the §3.3 line).
- **6 grid-floor failures** — seeds 0, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9: `kstar_model = 0.006678` identically
  (the k-grid floor, not a distinct physical answer), `kstar_fft_rel_err = 0.97687`.
- The outcome is **bimodal, not a spread**: no seed lands between the floor and the interior
  peak. This is a restart-selection artefact on one fixed target, which is exactly why
  best-of-64 restarts (not best-of-1) is the reported recovery protocol throughout — see
  `docs/DIAGNOSTICS_lift.md` D5 for the underlying diagnosis (`analysis.json::turing_flags`,
  `kstar_at_grid_floor`).

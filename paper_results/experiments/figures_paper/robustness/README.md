# Robustness benchmark + lift-invariance — paper figures

Two figures for the paper, generated by `make_robustness_figures.py` from tracked run
records (no re-running of trainers or rollouts). Full number-by-number provenance is in
`caption_data.md`.

## `figures/1_robustness_benchmark.png` — claim 4, ROBUSTNESS BENCHMARK

The 127-system generator population's own local Turing-volume distribution at four
perturbation levels (1 %, 4.8 %, 10 %, 20 %), with the 16 recovered `c2_P` models'
`turing_volume_4p8pct` / `turing_volume_10pct` overlaid. Panel (a) is "the §3.2 bars" —
population median/mean/worst per noise level plus the two pre-registered pass bars
(0.90 at 10 %, 0.95 at 4.8 %) — with the recovered models scattered on top. Panel (b) is
the headline comparison at 10 % noise: recovered median (1.000) against the population's
own median (0.935), the population being the reference class the recovered models are
judged against, not zero.

**Headline:** recovered median `turing_volume_10pct` (1.000) ≥ population median (0.935);
both pre-registered pass bars (§3.2) are cleared.

## `figures/2_lift_invariance.png` — claim 2, LIFT-INVARIANCE

For the four **learned** models `d5/seed{1,3,5,6}` (n = 4): perturbation-cloud Turing
volume and mean surviving `k*` against the timescale-separation parameter `mu`, across the
biological band `[1.1e-5, 9.2e-3]` (D-LIFT-1). This is **report-only** —
`docs/PREREGISTRATION.md` §3.7 states the robustness-vs-`mu` row is reported, never gated;
§3.2's bar is drawn only for visual reference against a different, harder criterion (the
finite-`mu` lifted Jacobian rather than QSS), not as a pass condition for this figure.

**Headline:** the four models are lift-invariant (volume cost 2–12 percentage points
across the band; `k*` essentially flat), while sitting well below §3.2's absolute
robustness bar (0.29–0.43 vs 0.90) — the claim is invariance under the lift, not absolute
robustness. Aggregated across all 27 QSS-Turing models evaluated in this V4 run (the 4
learned models plus 23 harvested generator-population models), P(lifted-Turing across the
band | QSS-Turing) = 27/27 = 1.000 (Wilson 95 % lower bound 0.8754).
